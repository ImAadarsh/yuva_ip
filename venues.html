Venue Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

You can select the venue based on geographic proximity, thematic focus that aligns with your interests, or specific dates that work with your schedule. While each venue has a unique focus area, all thematic discussions will be available at each location.

Accommodation is not included in the standard registration. However, we have partnered with nearby hotels to provide discounted rates for YUVA participants. Details about accommodation options will be shared upon successful registration.

Delegates for the National Summit will be selected based on their contributions during the regional symposia. This includes the quality of their position papers, active participation in discussions, innovative ideas presented, and collaborative spirit demonstrated. Each regional symposium will nominate representatives to attend the National Summit.
